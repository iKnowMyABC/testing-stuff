📌 OPERATIONAL RULES & GUIDELINES

1. Separate Daily Lists

Each day operates on a completely separate Conductor and VIP selection pool.

2. Duplicate Protection

If a drawn player was already scheduled on that same day last week, the next candidate is selected.

3. Weekly Compilation

The train schedule is generated every Sunday taking into account current weekly performance and results.

4. Schedule Publication

The finalized list is posted Sunday evening or Monday morning so all members know the roster in advance.
